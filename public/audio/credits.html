Audio sources and credits

These sounds provide an interpretive atmosphere. They are modern recordings and sound effects, not recordings of London in 1854 or additional historical evidence.

Wooden wagon

WWS Woodenwagon.ogg — Work With Sounds / Museum of Municipal Engineering; sound recordist Monika Widzicka (2015). Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

The source records a modern educational wooden wagon on pavement at a museum in Szreniawa, Poland, pushed by two people. It is used here as an illustrative distant cart. Changes: excerpt from 1–13 seconds, mono conversion, filtering, resampling, level adjustment and fades; playback is spatialized and varied in speed.

Recorded foley

Kenney: Impact Sounds and RPG Audio, licensed under CC0 1.0. Selected wood and hard-surface footsteps, wood impacts, book pages, fabric, creaks, metal vessels and a door. Changes: mono conversion, filtering, resampling, level adjustment and edge fades. Hard-surface footstep recordings are an interpretive stand-in for paving.

Original license notices: Impact Sounds, RPG Audio. Individual files, source links, modifications and checksums.

Pump water

Pump water: Water flowing, pouring, trickling by stephan / PDSounds (2007), released into the public domain by its author. A modern basin-to-bucket recording represents the pump's water burst. Changes: excerpt 30–30.95 seconds, mono conversion, filtering, resampling, DC removal, normalization and edge fades.

Original generated sounds

Soft air, fallback cart/hoof and water impressions, and interaction cues are generated by Broad Street Inquiry. No music or intelligible background speech is included.
