# John Snow's south London water-supply map

- Work: **Map 2**, from John Snow, *On the Mode of Communication of Cholera*, second edition, 1855. Depicts the Lambeth and Southwark and Vauxhall water supplies, including their intermingled service area.
- Image credit: **Wellcome Library, London**, image **L0063432**.
