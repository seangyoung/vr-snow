Adapted for The Broad Street Inquiry: simplified mesh, resized wood color/normal
textures, normalized dimensions, new iron material, added bungs, placement and
baked room lighting. Original presentation scene and OpenFootage metal textures
are not included. The original supplied license is archived in
assets/wooden-barrel/BLENDSWAP_LICENSE.txt in the source repository.

Broad Street pump detail additions are original project geometry. The separate
workhouse pump adaptation is credited in workhouse-hand-pump.txt.
