Only profiles used by the app's known controller mappings and generic controller
fallback profiles are included. GLB files are unchanged; profile assetPath values
point to deduplicated, content-addressed local files. Reproduce the export with
scripts/assets/sync-controller-assets.mjs and an unpacked copy of the package.

Models portray their manufacturers' hardware and trademarks for accurate device
representation. These trademarks do not imply endorsement of this experience.
