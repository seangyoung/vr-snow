# Household material provenance

The images in `textures/` are original deterministic procedural textures generated by `scripts/assets/household_details.py`. They contain no downloaded photographs, scans, or third-party texture assets. The wallpaper is an interpretive geometric repeat, not a documented historic pattern. Base colour and roughness are 512 px; normal maps are 256 px.

See `docs/household-environment-pass.md` for the furnishing references, rebuild commands, and validation limits.
