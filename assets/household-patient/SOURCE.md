# Resting household figure

The face, eyes, eyebrows, and hair derive from the project's existing MakeHuman-based registrar authoring source. See `assets/scene-people/SOURCE.md` and its upstream asset/license records. The build report records the source hash. The nightshirt and occupied coverlet are original generated geometry from `scripts/assets/build_household_patient.py`.

This is an unnamed composite father with cholera, not a portrait or reconstruction of a documented household. His daughter supplies the interview; the figure does not grant evidence. The bed's pillow remains fixed. The coverlet's small runtime movement is disabled by reduced motion.
