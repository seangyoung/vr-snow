# Interior visual pass — September 2026

This pass prioritizes Snow's office, the registrar and the household after the Broad Street exterior finish. The furniture and plaster in these rooms were mostly flat colors, so shared surface detail offered a useful improvement without adding scene complexity.

- Muted timber grain on furniture, doors and floorboards, with grain aligned to each piece's longest face dimension.
- Subtle plaster, paper, leather and cloth variation; a restrained geometric woven rug in the office.
- Offline vertex ambient occlusion around furniture, shelves and architectural intersections. Sixteen deterministic hemisphere rays per sampled corner darken nearby recesses; this is an approximation, not a full lightmap.
- Lower office fireplace fill and more neutral daylight to reduce the bright wall hotspot.
- Smooth shading on the existing household jug, cup and basin, with a softer ceramic sheen.
- Original rug border strips removed in favor of the textured border.

The finishes are original numerical patterns generated by `scripts/assets/interior_finish.py`. They contain no downloaded imagery and make no claim to reproduce a documented historical fabric, wallpaper or furnishing. These remain interpretive rooms. Texture PNGs live in `assets/interior-finish/textures`; the three room builders regenerate them and the packed, editable Blender files. Vertex shading is stored in the `Color` mesh attribute and explicitly exported as glTF `COLOR_0`.

The later [office and registrar identity pass](office-and-registrar-identity.md) changes those two layouts and their asset counts. The figures below record this initial surface-finish pass.
